# Disclaimer

**QuantRank500 is a record of predictions, not investment advice.**

- Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
- Statistics reflect verified prediction accuracy against exchange trade
  records. They do not represent actual trading returns.
- Settlements are computed from exchange trade records (tick data, with
  1-minute bars as a documented fallback). Actual execution prices may differ
  due to spread, commissions, and fill timing.
- QuantRank500 verifies predictions, not executions. It does not verify that
  any participant traded real capital.
- The $500 account is simulated. Past prediction accuracy does not predict
  future results.

ledger data is [CC0](DATA_LICENSE.md).
